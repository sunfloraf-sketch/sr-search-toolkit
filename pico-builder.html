中文同義詞僅供規劃參考，不納入產出的檢索式。英文同義詞建議先用「鄰近字組合器」整理好再貼入。控制詞彙一格可放多個，一行一個（詞內逗號會保留，如 Diabetes, Gestational）。對應：MeSH→MEDLINE(各平台)·PubMed·Cochrane；Emtree→Embase(各平台)；CINAHL Headings→CINAHL(EBSCOhost)；WoS·Scopus 無控制詞彙。Chinese synonyms are for planning only and are not included in the output. Tidy English synonyms in the Proximity Builder first. One controlled term per line (commas inside a term are kept, e.g. Diabetes, Gestational). Mapping: MeSH→MEDLINE(all platforms)·PubMed·Cochrane; Emtree→Embase(all platforms); CINAHL Headings→CINAHL(EBSCOhost); WoS·Scopus have none.

三級欄位對應（引擎使用中的規則） Three field levels (rules used by the engine)
L1 完整（SR）：自由詞 = 標題+摘要+關鍵字；控制詞 = 展開（含下位詞）。
L2 平衡：自由詞 = 標題+關鍵字；控制詞 = 展開。
L3 精準：自由詞 = 僅標題；控制詞 = 主標目（major）。
各庫實際欄位語法依《檢索語法大全》對照表自動套用；Web of Science 與 Scopus 無控制詞彙。
L1 (SR): free-text = title+abstract+keyword; controlled = exploded.
L2: free-text = title+keyword; controlled = exploded.
L3: free-text = title only; controlled = major heading.
Web of Science and Scopus have no controlled vocabulary.
此工具在您的瀏覽器本機執行，不上傳任何資料。產出的檢索策略仍請人工覆核並於資料庫實測後再使用。 Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Always human-check and test the output in each database before use.
