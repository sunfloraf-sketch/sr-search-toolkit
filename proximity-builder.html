① 自動分堆（選用）：貼上 Before 清單① Auto-split (optional): paste a Before list

每行一個多字詞。按「自動分堆」後合併字根、保留縮寫，並用二部圖著色分到兩堆（3–4 堆時先分兩堆，其餘請手動移）。被移除或未分類的字會列在下方，可加回任一堆。停用字僅含純虛詞（介系詞/冠詞/連接詞），疾病/症狀等實詞不會被當停用字。
One multi-word term per line. Auto-split merges roots, keeps abbreviations, and splits into two stacks by bipartite coloring (for 3–4 stacks it fills two, move the rest manually). Removed/unclassified words are listed below and can be added back to any stack. Stopwords are function words only (prepositions/articles/conjunctions); disease/symptom terms are never treated as stopwords.

② 詞堆（可手動編輯）② Stacks (editable)

提示：每堆是鄰近關係的一個位置。距離依來源資料庫自動換算（Ovid/Embase/Cochrane 需 +1）。
Tip: each stack is one position of the proximity relation. Distance is auto-adjusted per database (Ovid/Embase/Cochrane +1).
此工具在瀏覽器本機執行，不上傳資料。自動分堆為輔助建議，請務必人工核對切截點與分堆。 Runs locally; nothing is uploaded. Auto-split is only a suggestion — always verify truncation points and grouping.
